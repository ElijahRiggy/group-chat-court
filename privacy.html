What you submit

When you file a case, whatever you paste into the evidence box (and/or the screenshot you attach) is sent to our AI provider to generate a ruling. We don't create accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email, or other identifying information to use the site.

Where that evidence goes

Filing a case sends your submitted text and/or image to:

  • Groq (groq.com) — processes the evidence and generates the verdict text. See Groq's own privacy policy for how they handle API requests.

We don't store your submitted evidence in a database on our end. It exists only for the length of that one request to generate your verdict.

Payments

If you use the "Object to the Ruling" feature, payment is handled entirely by Stripe (stripe.com) on their own hosted checkout page — we never see or store your card details. We verify with Stripe, server-side, whether a payment succeeded before applying it, using only the checkout session's payment status. If you send a tip, that's handled the same way by Ko-fi (ko-fi.com).

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies & local storage

Aside from any cookies set by AdSense, this site uses your browser's local storage only to temporarily hold an in-progress case while you complete a payment on Stripe, so it can be refiled automatically when you return. That data stays on your device and is cleared automatically once used.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.
